Menstrual Hygiene
Workshop

Our menstrual hygiene workshops provide a safe, supportive, and non-judgmental environment where students can openly learn about the biological facts of menstruation. Through interactive discussions and educational activities, participants gain a better understanding of how the menstrual cycle works, why it happens, and how to manage it in a healthy and confident way.

The workshops also focus on breaking common myths and reducing the stigma surrounding menstruation, encouraging students to ask questions and share their thoughts without embarrassment. In addition, we teach practical menstrual hygiene practices, including the proper use and disposal of sanitary products, maintaining personal hygiene, and understanding how menstruation relates to overall health and well-being.

Menstrual
Bracelet Workshop

In this workshop, participants make a menstrual bracelet using color-coded beads that represent the different hormonal phases of the menstrual cycle. Each color symbolizes a stage, helping students visually understand how hormones change throughout the month and how these changes affect the body, mood, and energy levels.

Through this hands-on activity, participants learn about the four main phases of the menstrual cycle and how to track them in a simple and meaningful way. The bracelet serves as both an educational tool and a personal reminder that menstruation is a natural biological process.

By combining creativity with science-based learning, the workshop encourages open conversation about menstrual health, builds body awareness, and helps participants feel more confident in understanding their own cycles.

Sustainable
Product Workshop

In this workshop, participants are introduced to sustainable menstrual products through hands-on demonstrations and guided discussions. We showcase alternatives such as menstrual cups and reusable pads, explaining how they work, how to use them safely, and how to properly clean and care for them.

The session also highlights the environmental impact of disposable menstrual products and encourages participants to think about more eco-friendly choices. By comparing different options, students learn how sustainable products can reduce waste, lower long-term costs, and promote healthier menstrual practices.

Through open discussion and practical demonstrations, the workshop aims to empower participants with knowledge about sustainable menstrual care, helping them make informed decisions that are both beneficial for their personal health and for the environment.
